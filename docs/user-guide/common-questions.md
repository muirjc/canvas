# Common questions

## Why does my new diagram say "Not saved"?

A new diagram, whether from **New Diagram**, an import, a template or **Create with AI**, isn't
saved until you click **Save**. Until then only you can see it, and it isn't in the project's list.
If you leave and choose **Discard and leave**, it's deleted. See
[Creating diagrams](creating-diagrams.md).

## Where did the diagram I just made go?

If you left it without saving and chose **Discard and leave**, it was deleted, as if it had never
been made. A new diagram left behind when a browser tab closed is removed after a day. Saved
diagrams that someone deleted can be restored by an admin for 30 days.

## Why is a shape flagged on the Issues tab?

Your organisation's **standard** for the diagram type says how diagrams should look: which kinds of
element to use, their shapes and colours, how many there may be, and which connections are allowed.
The **Issues** tab lists where the diagram doesn't follow it. **Must fix** is a rule to meet,
**Warning** something to look at, **Info** a suggestion. Issues never stop you saving. Click one to
select the shapes it's about. See [The Issues tab](editor.md#the-issues-tab-standards).

## Why can't I add another element of a kind?

The standard limits how many a diagram may have. A kind's button is greyed out once the diagram has
its maximum, and its tooltip says why. Some kinds are counted together, for example one
**Component** whether it's New, Reused or Retired: once you have one of them, the others are greyed
out too. Delete one, or change its kind, to add another.

## What does "External" mean on a C4 element kind?

In C4, an **external** element is something outside the system you're describing, such as another
company's system or a person outside your organisation. Canvas draws it in the C4 external style
(`System_Ext`, `Person_Ext` and so on). A standard can treat internal and external elements as
different kinds, with their own colours.

## How do I get edit access to a diagram or project?

Everyone signed in can read every diagram. To change one you need **Edit** on it or its project.
Ask the project's owner, or someone with **Manage** on it, to add you on the project's **Members**
list. Your admin can help too. Viewers can comment at most. See
[Sharing and exporting](sharing-and-exporting.md).

## Why can't I pin a comment?

**Pin to** needs exactly one shape, connector or group selected on the canvas. Select one and the
option turns on. See [Comments](editor.md#comments).

## Why is Export greyed out?

Export always uses the last **saved** version, so it waits until you save your changes.

## Someone else saved the diagram while I was editing. What now?

Canvas stops your save and tells you who saved and when. You can **Save mine anyway** (theirs stays
in History), **Load the newer version**, or **Keep editing**. You can also copy or download your
version first. See [When someone else is editing](editor.md#when-someone-else-is-editing).

## How do I see more of a big diagram?

Use the zoom controls at the bottom-left of the canvas, or hold **Ctrl** (**Cmd** on a Mac) and
scroll. **Fit** shows the whole diagram. See [Zoom](editor.md#zoom).
